    A little about these days

    A handpicked daily trio of food days, playful traditions and celebrations from around the world. Some days invite a thoughtful moment, too. These are informal observances, not official days off.

    Dates checked with Days Of The Year; selected dates cross-checked with timeanddate. Each holiday’s source is below. Short descriptions are written for this app.

    Only fixed dates are included, so this calendar repeats every year, including 2027 and 2028. Floating days such as Thanksgiving and the first Friday of June are left out. February 29 gets a little leap-day greeting.

    No account, ads, tracking or forms. Nothing is collected by the app. Share opens your phone’s sharing choices; nothing is sent until you choose where.

    On iPhone: open the browser’s Share menu, then choose Add to Home Screen. Wait for “Ready for offline days” once before going offline. Your phone can occasionally clear saved website data.

    Small celebrations. A whole year of possibilities.
